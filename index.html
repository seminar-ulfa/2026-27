Odzivni teritoriji Responsive Territories

Urbanistični studio, ki industrijska območja ob letališčih, pristaniščih in večjih železniških vozliščih bere kot del mesta. Letos poteka v okviru projekta HubCities: najprej razvijemo metodo na evropskem merilu, nato jo prenesemo na ljubljanski obroč in v njem umestimo novo rabo. An urban design studio that reads industrial land around airports, seaports and major rail hubs as part of the city. This year it runs within the HubCities project: we first develop a method at European scale, then transfer it to the Ljubljana ring and place a new use there.

1.1MetodaMethod

Od sloja CLC do vozliščaFrom the CLC layer to a hub

  1. Samo razred 121Class 121 only

    Delamo izključno na industrijskih in poslovnih enotah (CLC 121), ne na celotnem sloju rabe tal. Leto 1990 izpustimo, ker ne pokriva vseh držav.We work only with industrial or commercial units (CLC 121), not the full land-cover layer. 1990 is left out because it does not cover all countries.

  2. Štirje deli EvropeFour parts of Europe

    Evropa je razdeljena po regijah NUTS 1; vsaka študentka obdela svoj del po isti metodi.Europe is split by NUTS 1 regions; each student processes her part with the same method.

  3. Vplivni pas 5 km5 km buffer

    Pas je narisan okoli poligona letališča oziroma pristanišča, ne okoli središčne točke. Znotraj pasov izberemo površine 121; tako nastane HubCity.The buffer is drawn around the airport or port polygon, not a centre point. Class 121 inside the buffers forms a HubCity.

  4. SuperHubCity

    Kjer se pasovi dveh ali več tipov vozlišč prekrivajo, nastane SuperHubCity. Tip zapišemo s kombinacijo: LP, LŽ, PŽ, LPŽ (letališče, pristanišče, železnica).Where buffers of two or more hub types overlap, a SuperHubCity emerges. Its type records the combination: AP, AR, PR, APR (airport, port, rail).

  5. Delež pozidanostiBuilt-up share

    delež = površina 121 v območju / površina območja × 100, za CLC 2018. Rezultat je tabela z imenom, tipom, površino območja, površino 121 in deležem.share = area of 121 in the area / area × 100, for CLC 2018. The result is a table with name, type, area, 121 area and share.

  6. Trije tipični primeriThree typical cases

    Območja razvrstimo po deležu in izberemo visok, srednji in nizek primer (orientacijsko 20, 10 in 5 %), z utemeljitvijo, zakaj niso izjeme.Areas are ranked by share and a high, medium and low case is chosen (roughly 20, 10 and 5 %), with a note on why they are typical rather than outliers.

  7. Progresija iz slojev spremembProgression from change layers

    Rast 121 po obdobjih 2000–2006, 2006–2012 in 2012–2018 iz slojev CLC Change, ne z odštevanjem statusnih slojev, ki daje šum ob robovih poligonov. Za vsako obdobje nove površine v ha in rastni indeks glede na 2000; sloj prog s poljem obd 0–3.Growth of 121 for 2000–2006, 2006–2012 and 2012–2018 from the CLC Change layers, not by subtracting status layers, which creates noise along polygon edges. For each period new area in ha and a growth index relative to 2000; a prog layer with field obd 0–3.

  8. Enotna predstavitevShared presentation

    Vse karte izhajajo iz paketa hubcities_reduced.ppkx: industrija #A80084, pas letališča rumen, pas pristanišča moder, obdobja v magenta lestvici. Navodila so v receptu SLO/ENG (2. 11. 2026).All maps start from the hubcities_reduced.ppkx package: industry #A80084, airport buffer yellow, port buffer blue, periods in a magenta ramp. Steps are in the SLO/ENG recipe (2 Nov 2026).

2.3.1MetodologijaMethod

Kako beremo referenčni primerHow we read a reference project

Vsak primer opišemo po istih kategorijah, da so primeri med seboj in z lokacijo v ljubljanskem obroču primerljivi. Kartice v zbirki kažejo prvi dve kategoriji; ostale se dopolnjujejo v procesu posamezne naloge.Every case is described with the same categories so that cases can be compared with each other and with a site in the Ljubljana ring. The cards show the first two; the rest are added within each task's process.

  1. Tehnična rabaTechnical use

    Kaj objekt proizvaja, predeluje ali hrani in kakšne obratovalne zahteve ima.What the building produces, processes or stores, and what it needs to operate.

  2. Javna rabaPublic use

    Kateri program je dodan, komu je namenjen in kako je dostopen.Which programme is added, for whom, and how it is accessed.

  3. Tokovi in sinergijeFlows and synergies

    Toplota, energija, voda in material med tehnično rabo, javno rabo in sosednjimi obrati.Heat, energy, water and material exchanged between the technical use, the public use and neighbouring plants.

  4. MeriloScale

    Tloris, bruto površina in višina, primerjani z velikostjo industrijskih območij v obroču.Footprint, gross floor area and height, compared with the size of industrial areas in the ring.

  5. Odnos do mestaRelation to the city

    Lega v tkivu, robovi, dostop in pritličje: kako se tehnična stavba poveže z okolico.Position in the urban fabric, edges, access and ground floor: how the technical building connects to its surroundings.

  6. Prenos v LjubljanoTransfer to Ljubljana

    Kaj je prenosljivo na izbrano lokacijo v obroču in kaj ne.What can be transferred to the chosen site in the ring, and what cannot.
